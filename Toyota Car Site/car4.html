Toyota Corolla Key Specifications



  • Powertrain Hybrid-Only: All trims now come standard with the fifth-generation Toyota Hybrid System (THS 5).
  • Engine: 2.0-liter 4-cylinder engine
  • Power / Torque: 87.5 bhp with ~205 Nm torque
  • Transmission: Continuously Variable Transmission (CVT)
  • Fuel Efficiency: Approx. 30-35 km/l (varies based on driving conditions)
  • Drive Type: Front-Wheel Drive (FWD)


Features & Comfort



  • Infotainment System: 9-inch touchscreen with Apple CarPlay and Android Auto.
  • Sound System: JBL premium audio system with multiple speakers.
  • Climate Control: Dual-zone automatic climate control.
  • Seating: Leather upholstery, power-adjustable front seats, and heated front seats.
  • Sunroof: Available in higher trims.
